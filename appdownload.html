Connect Flower to your phone.

Send books, change settings, update the firmware — all from your phone. No Google Play, no App Store. Install it straight from this page.

1

Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or tap the button below.

2

Install the app. Android may warn about installing from outside the Play Store — that's expected, just confirm.

3

On the reader: Settings → Connect app. Join the Wi-Fi network that shows up on your phone.

4

Open the Flower app — it finds your reader automatically.
